## 2. Terminology and assurance boundaries

| Term | Meaning in this document |
| --- | --- |
| Code-enforced guarantee | A property of the genuine inspected implementations, subject to their build and external dependencies. Example: `retract` cannot burn a top receipt held by another address. Source inspection is not proof that a production address runs that code. |
| Operator-enforced procedure / operational mitigation | A required human/tooling workflow that direct contract calls may bypass. Examples: independent allocation review, withholding receipts from outsiders before finalization, and proving genuine deployed code. |
| Intended economic policy | A project choice, such as restricting admission to a fixed whitelist to protect intended allocation weights or declining an administrative rescue power. A policy is not necessarily fully enforced by code. |
| Unverified deployment assumption | A claim about actual addresses, owners, miners, receipts, beneficiaries, transaction ordering or launch state for which this task has no verified production evidence. It remains **NOT VERIFIED**. |
| Formal admission / custody opening | A successful `depositMiners` or `registerMiners` call. The first success sets `custodyOpen`. Physical NFT ownership can reach the Pool earlier by an unsupported plain transfer; the finality gate does not prevent that transfer. The proposed production procedure supports only atomic `depositMiners`. |
| Receipt claims versus BEM claims | Distributor `claim`/`deliver` transfers allocated receipts after allocation finalization. Pool `claim`/`claimBatch` pays BEM to current receipt holders and requires Pool activation. Opening receipt claims does not imply that mining income exists or that every beneficiary has claimed. |
| Risk acceptance | Explicit acceptance of a stated residual risk for an identified release and workflow. Writing this proposal, retaining code unchanged, or passing fixture tests does not constitute approval. |

Source for the admission/claim distinction: [Pool entry points](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L333-L355), [BEM claims](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L415-L439), [Distributor claims](../src/TapeoutReceiptDistributor.sol#L170-L197), and [custody gate](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L623-L631).

### 3.1 ORIGINAL SALUS #1 (Medium): Rewards locked when all circuits are revoked before registration

**Original reference:** SALUS PDF page 7, printed page 6. **Owner disposition:** operational risk accepted under the confirmed admission workflow. The owner has confirmed the initial custody route described below. **Actual deployment/execution evidence and SALUS confirmation remain NOT VERIFIED**; no code fix or auditor closure is claimed.

**Trigger and impact.** A miner reaches the Pool without registration, then becomes ineligible before a later `registerMiners`. In SALUS's scenario every directly transferred miner is revoked before any is registered; revocation pays accrued BEM to the Pool, but no admissible miner remains to establish composition. The Pool requires ACTIVE status and cannot admit a revoked miner. The terminal status/payment behavior is an external-protocol premise recorded in [Mining lifecycle](MINING_LIFECYCLE.md#L21-L61), not freshly probed here.

The resulting cases must be distinguished:

- An NFT transferred or minted directly to the Pool, where the external collection permits that route, is not registered merely by being Pool-held. Standard safe ERC-721 transfers requiring a receiver hook fail because this Pool has no such hook; plain transfers can still create the unsupported window. The genuine Pool has no outbound miner transfer, withdrawal, rescue, sweep or arbitrary-call path. An unadmittable NFT can remain inaccessible in Pool custody indefinitely.
- BEM received while `totalNandBurn + totalLatchBurn == 0` remains held but unrecognized. It creates no payout entitlement at that time. If no valid first admission ever succeeds, it remains locked under the genuine implementation. SALUS's exhausted-materials/all-miners-revoked case is one way this can become permanent.
- If a valid miner is later admitted, the Pool first attributes the newly admitted miner's measured carried payment, then adds its burn and recognizes the previously held BEM as **ordinary income under the first composition**. It does not attribute that balance to the revoked/unregistered miner or reimburse its sender.
- If composition already exists, BEM paid for an unregistered miner is recognized under the composition at recognition, including the old-composition sync before a later registration. Later registration cannot undo that attribution window.

These are custody/claimability losses, not evidence that the NFT or BEM was burned. A later valid admission is not a recovery promise: eligibility, finality, whitelist access, external calls and gas must still permit it.

**Relevant source.** [Fixed whitelist](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L267-L277); [deposit/register semantics](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L333-L355); [admission validation, transfer and registry](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L623-L698); [carried-income measurement](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L704-L735); [ACTIVE/composition checks](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L755-L772); [zero-composition sync](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L868-L881); [accounting recognition](ACCOUNTING.md#L13-L31). The complete production Pool exposes only entitlement/role BEM payments, with [payment internals](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L827-L912), and no administrative asset extraction.

**Owner-confirmed workflow and economic rationale.** Admission is intentionally restricted to the Pool's fixed depositor whitelist so arbitrary outside miners with materially different NAND/LATCH composition cannot enter and change allocation weights. The whitelist governs callers; it does not itself authenticate project provenance or prescribe a target ratio. Authorized depositors must select the intended miners. The owner has confirmed that production miners will first be held by one fixed whitelisted depositor address, approved for the Pool, and admitted only with `depositMiners`, atomically transferring, registering and accounting for them. No production miner will be directly minted or separately transferred to the Pool for later registration. The exact address, deployed whitelist and actual holdings still require evidence.

**Controls enforced by code.** The caller must be whitelisted; for `depositMiners` it must own each NFT. Each miner must meet ACTIVE, identity, burn-cache and supported-category checks. Transfer, registration and accounting are in one transaction; failure reverts those changes. `registerMiners` has admission checks but cannot make the earlier transfer/mint atomic with registration.

**Controls dependent on operations.** Before any production miner reaches the Pool, verify that the actual mint/build destination and subsequent holder, the depositor's control and whitelist membership, and the exact admission payload match the confirmed atomic route. Do not transfer or mint directly to the Pool for later registration. The project does not promise recovery of unsolicited/unsupported transfers and does not propose an arbitrary administrative sweep, withdrawal or rescue power.

**Residual risk and invalidation.** Unsupported assets may be permanently inaccessible; pre-registration proceeds may be locked or allocated to existing receipt/role entitlements rather than the sender. Authorized-depositor error remains possible. The owner accepts these residual operational risks under the confirmed workflow. Acceptance is invalid if the workflow initially leaves production miners Pool-held, requires a separate direct transfer/mint followed by registration, cannot use an authorized holder, or expects administrative recovery. Any such deviation requires reassessment; the owner confirmation does not remove the unsupported code path or guarantee recovery.

**Confirmation and required evidence.** **The owner has confirmed the initial-holder and atomic-admission policy; that policy question is no longer unanswered.** Actual deployment/execution verification remains pending: obtain the exact depositor address, collection and token IDs; holder and whitelist evidence; ACTIVE status, burn/cache and category checks; approvals and reviewed `depositMiners` payload; and pre-admission Pool NFT/BEM inventory at an identified chain/block. Retain admission transaction evidence showing that the confirmed route was followed. Inspect the actual BEM balance and [reconciliation](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L564-L585): [`unrecognizedBalance()`](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L510-L516) returns zero before composition and cannot by itself prove that no BEM is held. No actual deployment, holdings or admission transaction is verified by this owner confirmation. SALUS's official disposition remains pending confirmation.

### 3.2 ORIGINAL SALUS #4 (Low): Unbounded wallet revocation can prevent allocation corrections

**Original reference:** SALUS PDF page 10, printed page 9. **Owner disposition:** operational risk accepted for limited pre-finalization correction capacity. The unbounded operation remains; actual allocation/execution evidence and SALUS confirmation remain **NOT VERIFIED**.

**Trigger and impact.** `revokeWallet(wallet)` must traverse every allocation range and every ID for that wallet in a single transaction. Allocations can accumulate through many calls. A sufficiently large erroneous allocation may exceed transaction gas limits when revoked, preventing correction before finalization. A per-range bound does not bound a wallet's cumulative allocation.

**Relevant source.** [`allocate` and range accumulation](../src/TapeoutReceiptDistributor.sol#L122-L148); [`revokeWallet`](../src/TapeoutReceiptDistributor.sol#L151-L168); [separate bounded receipt delivery path](../src/TapeoutReceiptDistributor.sol#L272-L298); [historical operator gas guidance](OPERATIONS.md#L192-L198).

**Owner-confirmed workflow.** Normal operation does not rely on `revokeWallet`. It clears a wallet's allocation records for setup correction; it does not revoke an administrator role or retract issued receipts. Correct issuance and correct allocation need no such call. Review the contribution/allocation JSON before funding/import, then independently reconcile actual beneficiaries, ranges and complete supply before finalization; finish required setup and then renounce the relevant administrative authority. Correct total issuance alone does not establish correct beneficiaries. Stop on discrepancies and follow section 5.3 if the existing setup cannot be corrected.

**Controls enforced by code.** Revocation is owner-only and unavailable after `allocationFinalized`. Allocation checks ownership of each ID and prevents duplicate allocation. There is no incremental or paged revocation. Receipt `claim(maxIds)`/`deliver` and Pool BEM batch claims are different functions. Frontend claim-size limits do not fix this finding.

**Controls dependent on operations.** Validate inputs before import, compare the actual imported records in both directions, retain every wallet's total IDs/ranges, and resolve any error before finalization. Non-use of revocation accepts reduced correction capacity; it does not ensure that no correction will be needed. Existing largest-wallet fixture results or approximate CLI thresholds are not gas proof for the final allocation.

**Residual risk and invalidation.** If a large allocation must be corrected, revocation may fail. A replacement distributor or broader replacement deployment may be necessary, subject to the actual setup state, and may not recover existing assets. The genuine distributor has no general inventory withdrawal, and the Pool's [binding is permanent](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L293-L306). Funding, binding, issuance/allocation finalization, deliveries and renunciation can remove options. Do not finalize known incorrect records or assume that redeployment is harmless. Acceptance must be revisited if a correction is required, the reviewed input changes, or operation depends on guaranteed revocation of large wallets.

**Confirmation and required evidence.** The owner accepts the absence of a guaranteed correction path and the residual risks above. Still required: reviewed JSON/artifact hashes, independently validated beneficiary records, on-chain ranges and per-wallet ID totals, full-supply reconciliation, and actual setup/binding/funding state. If correction capacity is relied upon for any wallet, require configuration-specific simulation/gas evidence before irreversible steps; none is supplied or newly measured here.

### 3.3 ORIGINAL SALUS #5 (Low): Ownership transfer can prevent correction of over-issuance

**Original reference:** SALUS PDF page 11, printed page 10. **Owner disposition:** operational risk accepted with a single issuance wallet and the documented supply-correction cutoff. Actual wallet, supply/correction evidence and SALUS confirmation remain **NOT VERIFIED**.

**Trigger and impact.** `retract(count)` requires both current ownership authority and possession of every required highest-numbered receipt. Ownership transfer alone does not transfer receipts. The new owner may therefore be unable to retract an over-issue. Keeping the same owner avoids this original ownership-handover scenario. The same possession condition also prevents retraction while the required top IDs are held elsewhere, including by the distributor, even if ownership authority has not changed. Correct issuance needs no retraction; normal funding does not itself create an over-issue.

**Relevant source.** [Issue/retract](../src/TapeoutEntitlementReceipt.sol#L57-L87); [issuance finalization and metadata](../src/TapeoutEntitlementReceipt.sol#L89-L99); [mint/burn prohibition after finalization](../src/TapeoutEntitlementReceipt.sol#L131-L150); [Distributor's only receipt delivery path](../src/TapeoutReceiptDistributor.sol#L276-L298).

**Owner-confirmed workflow.** Use one setup wallet and do not transfer ownership to another wallet during issuance. Complete supply review and all corrections while that issuer still holds the required highest-numbered IDs, **before finalizing issuance or transferring those receipts into the distributor**. The owner accepts that an over-issue discovered after the cutoff may be uncorrectable in that deployment. Section 5 provides a compatible sequence for both receipt categories and the conditions for rejecting an incorrect setup before production use.

**Controls enforced by code.** Retraction is owner-only, before issuance finalization, from the current highest IDs, each held by the caller, with at most `MAX_ISSUE_BATCH` (500) IDs per call. `finalizeIssuance` permanently disables minting and burning. Renunciation on the genuine implementation permanently removes owner-only authority. Receipt transfers remain possible after finalization; finalization does not freeze holders.

**Controls dependent on operations.** Keep issuance authority and required receipt possession together until correction is complete; verify owner/pending-owner state; approve exact supplies; complete metadata setup before renunciation. Issuing directly to the distributor is technically possible, but does not preserve an issuer-held retraction window and is not an equivalent correction procedure.

**Residual risk and invalidation.** Keeping the same owner avoids the ownership-handover scenario but cannot preserve retractability after required top IDs leave that wallet. The distributor cannot approve or arbitrarily return receipts to the setup wallet. Its delivery path opens only after both issuances are finalized and pays recorded beneficiaries, so it cannot restore pre-finalization retractability. Early funding, ownership transfer, missing top IDs, premature finalization or premature renunciation invalidates reliance on this mitigation. Correction after an irreversible step may be impossible; replacement is not an assured recovery method.

**Required evidence.** Identify the setup wallet and authority for each receipt contract; record owner/pendingOwner, issued supplies, intended counts, and top-ID holders before any correction; retain correction transactions and the final reconciliation; verify both `issuanceFinalized` flags and supplies before renunciation. No actual issuance wallet, production inventory or correction history has been verified here.

### 3.4 ORIGINAL SALUS #6 (Low): Unsolicited receipt transfers can delay allocation finalization

**Original reference:** SALUS PDF page 12, printed page 11. **Owner disposition:** operational risk accepted under exclusive control of supported receipts until allocation finalization and complete supply reconciliation. Actual holder/allocation/execution evidence and SALUS confirmation remain **NOT VERIFIED**.

**Trigger and impact.** Before allocation finalization, any holder can transfer supported receipts with unused Pool BEM claim history into the distributor. Inventory increases without an allocation. A holder can front-run finalization, making `allocated != inventory` and delaying it until the additional IDs are correctly allocated. Repeated transfers can repeat the delay. In 2.2.0, allocation finalization also gates Pool activation and therefore custody opening.

**Relevant source.** [Receiver hooks](../src/TapeoutReceiptDistributor.sol#L85-L114); [supported-contract and reward-history checks](../src/TapeoutReceiptDistributor.sol#L250-L269); [allocation finalization](../src/TapeoutReceiptDistributor.sol#L170-L184); [Pool activation](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L311-L327); [admission requires activation](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L619-L631).

**Owner-confirmed workflow.** Keep every supported receipt under the issuance wallet's control or in the intended genuine distributor until allocation finalization. Do not distribute supported receipts to external holders beforehand. Complete supply corrections before funding as required for #5; then fund the intended distributor with the complete issued supply and reconcile before finalization. The owner accepts that a deviation or unexpected receipt transfer can delay finalization, activation and admission; stop and reconcile rather than finalize an incorrect allocation.

**Controls enforced by code.** The distributor accepts only its specified NAND/LATCH receipt contracts, rejects non-unit positive amounts and IDs with Pool BEM claim history, and rejects subsequent deposits through both checked receiver paths once finalized. It does **not** enforce the Pool's depositor whitelist on receipt transfers. The token-contract check on `msg.sender` is not a sender whitelist: `from` is used for events, not authorization. Finalization requires both issuances finalized and `allocated == inventory` for each category, then permanently freezes allocation and opens receipt claims/delivery.

**Controls dependent on operations.** Prevent outsiders from possessing supported receipts during the vulnerable phase, including through unintended transfers or approvals. Reconcile complete issued supply, intended distributor inventory, all allocation records and the independently reviewed contribution/allocation JSON. Check exact IDs and beneficiaries, not only counts. The Pool does not enforce these procedures.

**Coverage requirement.** Immediately before finalization, for each category require `issued supply == cumulative accepted inventory == allocated IDs == reviewed intended total`, all IDs `1..S` present exactly once in the intended distributor/allocation, and no deliveries yet. The combined round must not be empty. An empty or partially funded distributor can satisfy `allocated == inventory` while leaving issued receipts elsewhere; that weaker equality is insufficient. After finalization, deliveries may occur before first miner admission: reconcile the full issued set as allocated IDs consisting of undelivered distributor holdings plus legitimately delivered IDs, with delivery/holder evidence. Cumulative inventory does not decrease on delivery. Current distributor balance alone is therefore also insufficient. See [SDK coverage and holdings checks](../sdk/verify.ts#L224-L289) and [reviewed-input verification](../sdk/verify.ts#L329-L395).

**Residual risk and invalidation.** The mitigation fails if outsiders hold supported receipts before finalization, supply remains outside the intended allocation, input/holder evidence is incomplete, or finalization is attempted on unreconciled state. A late receipt requires reconciliation, not arbitrary allocation merely to satisfy a counter. After finalization, wrong beneficiaries cannot be corrected and deposits through the checked hooks are refused. The [`finalize-allocation` CLI preflight](../cli/pool.ts#L406-L423) is an operational guard with a check-to-execution interval, and direct owner calls can bypass it. `--partial` is not acceptable evidence for the full-round policy proposed here.

**Required evidence.** All issued IDs and holders before finalization; transfer/approval history supporting exclusive control; exact reviewed contribution/allocation JSON and hashes; artifact and inventory provenance; every beneficiary/range; receiver compatibility; full supply/accepted/allocated/delivered/undelivered reconciliation; and finalization transaction/post-state. Actual launch inventory, holders, allocations and chain state remain **NOT VERIFIED**.

## 4. ORIGINAL SALUS #3: pre-custody distribution finality

**Original title: Centralization risk (Medium)**, SALUS PDF page 9, printed page 8. This is an additional launch-policy clarification, not a fifth assertion that a finding is closed.

The owner's stated intended policy is that **receipt allocation must be finalized, receipt claims must be open, and the genuine Distributor's ownership must be renounced before any production miner is formally admitted into permanent Pool custody**. It does not require all beneficiaries to have claimed their receipts. The policy also requires both genuine receipt contracts and the genuine Pool to have completed setup and renounced ownership before first admission.

The source enforces part of this dependency chain: [activation](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L311-L327) checks finalized issuance, exact supplies, a bound distributor and its finalized-allocation view; the [first-admission gate](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L744-L753) requires all four owner views to be zero. A successful first admission latches `custodyOpen`; a failed transaction rolls that change back. Neither activation nor renunciation is itself miner admission, and the Pool does not renounce other contracts on their behalf.

For the genuine implementations, finalized issuance/allocation cannot be reopened, and renunciation cannot be undone. All three contract types inherit `Ownable2Step`. The installed OpenZeppelin 5.4.0 source was inspected: `Ownable.renounceOwnership()` calls `_transferOwnership(address(0))`, and `Ownable2Step._transferOwnership()` deletes `_pendingOwner`. The dependency is pinned in [package.json](../package.json#L34) and [package-lock.json](../package-lock.json); [Fable P-7](INTERNAL_GROUPMINE_2_2_REVIEW_6345de8_FABLE51.md#L203-L218) separately records its historical pending-owner tests. **Read and retain `owner() == 0` and `pendingOwner() == 0` for all four actual contracts before admission.** Neither `_openCustody` nor [`readFinality`](../sdk/pool.ts#L451-L478) directly checks pendingOwner; this is an additional evidence requirement.

This policy removes post-setup owner-only issuance/allocation authority **only for genuine deployed implementations**, while preserving setup and allocation-correctness trust boundaries:

- The Pool's receipt constructor checks exact receipt runtime hashes and categories, but `bindDistributor` checks only code presence and reported Pool/receipt addresses. A lying or upgradeable look-alike can report finalized allocation and zero owner. Owner-zero alone does not authenticate Distributor code. See [constructor/binding](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L250-L306), Astra ST-01 (PDF page 21) and Fable P-7.
- The gate does not prove complete issued-supply coverage, truthful contributions, correct beneficiaries or a unique distributor. An empty/partial genuine distributor can finalize, and the existence of another distributor does not make it the intended bound one. Require complete inventory/allocation/delivery reconciliation independently.
- Do not treat `verify-deployment: MATCHES` or `READY_FOR_FIRST_ADMISSION` alone as authorization. The manifest permits an [optional distributor](../sdk/config.ts#L32-L38); [deployment verification](../sdk/deployment.ts#L93-L116) checks that contract and the Pool's bound address only inside that optional branch. ### 5.1 Separate ownership-renunciation cutoffs

| Contract | Complete before renunciation | What premature renunciation can prevent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NAND receipt | All NAND issue/retract decisions, `finalizeIssuance`, intended metadata (`setURI`) | Finalized issuance may become impossible; metadata correction is lost. |
| LATCH receipt | The same steps independently for LATCH | The same; finalizing or renouncing NAND does not finalize LATCH. |
| Distributor | All allocation review/correction and `finalizeAllocation` | Claims may never open and Pool activation may remain impossible; held receipts have no arbitrary recovery route. |
| Pool | Correct one-time `bindDistributor` and `activate` | Binding/activation may become impossible. Renouncing Distributor ownership does not perform either Pool action. |

Inherited ownership functions do not enforce these setup cutoffs; the [CLI renunciation preflight](../cli/pool.ts#L463-L478) is an operator guard. Once renounced, genuine owner-only setup cannot be resumed. Fable P-8 describes early-renunciation cost as redeployment without admitted custody; that statement must not be generalized into harmless recovery of already funded receipts, permanent bindings or unsupported direct transfers. Review the actual state before considering any replacement.

### 5.3 Pre-production error handling and replacement

The owner accepts a setup process that verifies correctness before production use, withholds funding when inputs are wrong, and may abandon an incorrect candidate and deploy fresh receipts/contracts. This does not guarantee that the existing deployment can be corrected or its assets recovered. Apply checks at each irreversible boundary, not only immediately before miner admission:

| Checkpoint | Required response to an error |
| --- | --- |
| Before receipt funding or issuance finalization | Review intended supplies and the off-chain allocation list; compare actual issued supply and issuer-held IDs. Correct an over-issue while `retract` is available, or reject the candidate. Withhold funding if inputs are wrong. No successful transaction is required merely to exercise a correction function when the inputs are already correct. |
| After funding/import, before allocation finalization | Compare the actual on-chain records with the independently reviewed list. A successful [`allocate`](../src/TapeoutReceiptDistributor.sol#L132-L138) already requires the IDs to be Distributor-held: an incorrect on-chain allocation is no longer an unfunded setup. Stop further finalization, renunciation and admission while assessing correction or replacement. Do not finalize known incorrect records. |
| If abandoning a candidate | Record existing receipt holders, inventory, allocations/deliveries, Pool-held assets, finalization and binding state. New issuance does not offset old issuance or cancel an old allocation. Replacing receipt contracts requires a Pool configured for those new receipts; its [receipt references are immutable](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L122-L123). A [bound Distributor cannot be replaced](../src/TapeoutMinerPool.sol#L293-L306). Depending on the current state, replacement may require a complete new contract set. Abandoning contracts does not retrieve, burn or invalidate their existing receipts/assets; recovery is not promised. |
| Before using a replacement for production | Identify the selected addresses and document the rejected addresses and any outstanding old receipts, deliveries or assets. Repeat code-identity, binding, full-supply, beneficiary and finality verification for the new candidate; update the reviewed manifest and artifacts. Prior checks do not transfer to new addresses. Keep production miners outside the Pool until all launch requirements pass. |

The stages have different consequences: receipt claims open immediately at allocation finalization, before first miner admission. Therefore, waiting until miner admission to discover an incorrect list is insufficient; allocation correctness must be established before claims open. Existing external holdings or delivered entitlements must be accounted for when evaluating a replacement. This acceptance concerns setup risk and does not authorize abandoning production assets or bypassing the separate release gates.

## 7. Proposed user disclosure

> Only eligible miners owned by an authorized depositor and admitted through the supported `depositMiners` transaction are supported. Sending or minting an NFT directly to the Pool does not register it. Unsupported transfers can result in irreversible asset loss or permanent locking; recovery and reimbursement are not promised. BEM received without any admitted composition may remain unrecognized indefinitely, or be allocated under a later first composition rather than to the sender. Assets are not described as burned merely because they are inaccessible.
>
> Miner custody is permanent under the genuine Pool implementation. There is no administrative miner withdrawal, arbitrary BEM sweep or rescue power. Supply and allocation corrections must be completed during setup; large allocation revocations may be impossible within gas limits. Top receipts are retractable only while held by the current issuer and before issuance finalization; the distributor provides no route to restore pre-finalization retractability. Finalization/renunciation removes further correction authority. Receipt claims open at allocation finalization and may remain unclaimed when mining starts; unclaimed receipts can continue accruing BEM, but delivery and BEM claiming remain subject to the genuine contracts and the beneficiary wallet's ability to receive and use them.

Publish the disclosure with the verified addresses, allocation evidence and approved operating policy before inviting production participation. This document records the proposed wording; publication and user acknowledgement have not been verified.
